**Research findings (details and sources in `01-research-findings.md`):**
- Harder generated problems are more often broken (answer-key accuracy fell 79% → 63% as difficulty rose). AI agreement is weak evidence; GPT-5 "proved" false statements 29% of the time.
- Novelty is the weakest point: ~23% of AI-set problems judged novel; even real AIME 2025 had near-copies online. Outputs converge to the same ideas.
- AI judges of problem quality correlate ~0.07 (quality) and ~0.11 (novelty) with experts. The only real ground truth is expert ratings.
- Feature-based difficulty prediction explains only ~20–55% of item difficulty and has not been validated for competition math. A creation-time vector is a **prior with wide variance**; observed per-learner data (Elo/Additive-Factors-style, drifting per-dimension skills, pooled by family) is what corrects it. Candidate dimensions: prerequisite load, breadth, solution length, computation load, insight/representation change, misdirection (learner-relative), representation, baseline AI solve rate (covariate only).
- Autoformalization (Lean) statement accuracy is ~45% and often subtly unfaithful: not available to Ben anyway (B2). Geometry: algebraic provers (Wu/Gröbner, Newclid) for equalities, draw diagrams from solved coordinates (AI-drawn diagrams compile ~36%). Fermi: log-error or interval scoring. Market-making: computable only under an explicit counterparty model.

**Seeds for the approaches (Claude's thinking so far, not decisions):**
- *Core-first (human setter method):* pick a technique/idea, hide it in a statement, then use the kit to verify and tune (clean integer answer in [0,999], unique, not guessable). Backwards-from-a-solution-idea gives slick solutions; forwards-by-mutation gives variety.
- *Combine/mutate:* take 1–3 bank problems, apply structured moves (generalise, specialise, invert, add a constraint, swap the counted object, merge two techniques), verify with the kit, run a novelty check against the bank (structure, not wording).
- *Tutor-driven targeting:* the tutor chooses one difficulty dimension to push (edge) or a strong area to deepen (depth), then asks for a family at that setting.
- Quality without an expert: validity gates (independent computation, uniqueness, unambiguity) → model-tier solve-rate ladder → anchored pairwise comparison against the 2000 real AMC problems with judges from different model families → Ben's blind-mixed behaviour → periodic expert ratings (B3 is one-time).
- Everything above must respect the abacus principles: **tool, not gate**; evidence honestly scoped; multi-project; nothing runs on the server.
